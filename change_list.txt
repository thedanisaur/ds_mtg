Adding to and updating set for inital testing.
The changes made are to help fill out the set, fix some flavor issues, and generally fix things.
  1. Removing fields on cards that aren't applicable to those cards.
  2. Gold creatures and spells were in the wrong files, fixed.
  3. Added this change list file to keep track of current changes, and as a template for commiting.
  4. Updated bleed wording so that creatures won't gain multiple instances of it.
  5. Added transform creature card list.
  6. Land subtype Fire updated to Bonfire.
  7. Added stubs for the enemy colored duals.
