Adding to and updating set for inital testing.
The changes made are to help fill out the set, fix some flavor issues, and generally fix things.
  1. I'd rather have a flyer in U than another big creature in Crystal Golem
  2. Removed Lifehunt Scythe because I don't think white needs that much removal.
  3. This commit finishes off the WUBRGL cards. Still need more Multicolored, Colorless, and Artifact cards.
